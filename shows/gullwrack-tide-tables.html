Gullwrack Tide Tables

Half hour of harbour folklore from the Reach: drowned bells, wool-weather, the ferryman who still rings twice. Read by the fishermen who claim to have seen them.

Sundays, 6:40 AM

Why you should start here

“Folklore you can set a watch by.” is how the continuity announcer introduces it, and she is right. Episodes are engineered to stand alone: arrive anywhere in the run, the prelude will put you right.

Recorded in one session to wire, with the full effects bench: the 1908 stair, a recovered ship's whistle, and a rain tray the size of a dinner table.

How to listen

Production notes
  • Recorded live to wire, one take, corrections broadcast as appended letters.
  • Engineered for standalone listening: the prelude reorients any new ear.
  • Archived complete since 1961 on lacquer and wire copy.
  • Broadcast at -3 dB relative to network standard, on director's orders.

Cast: three leads, rotating character pool of eleven.
