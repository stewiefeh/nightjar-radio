Signal Fires

A serial about the last radio operators of the Ashfall Years, relaying hope down a chain of burning towers. Recorded live to wire with a full effects bench.

Wednesdays, 9:15 PM

Why you should start here

“The towers burn in order. The message moves faster.” is how the continuity announcer introduces it, and she is right. Episodes are engineered to stand alone: arrive anywhere in the run, the prelude will put you right.

Recorded in one session to wire, with the full effects bench: the 1908 stair, a recovered ship's whistle, and a rain tray the size of a dinner table.

How to listen

Production notes
  • Recorded live to wire, one take, corrections broadcast as appended letters.
  • Engineered for standalone listening: the prelude reorients any new ear.
  • Archived complete since 1977 on lacquer and wire copy.
  • Broadcast at -3 dB relative to network standard, on director's orders.

Cast: three leads, rotating character pool of eleven.
