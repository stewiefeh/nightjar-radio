The Byre Gets a Licence

The secretaries apply for a broadcasting licence and are astonished to be taken seriously. The inspector is astonished to be charmed.

About this episode

Recorded live to wire in Studio A on the night of 2026-09-12. One take. The log records the weather, the kettle, and any dead air kept deliberately; all three columns are full.

Available to Listeners' Circle members on request by catalogue number. The digitisation queue runs eleven weeks; requests are answered in tide order.
