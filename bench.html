Effects bench: a partial catalogue

Desmond Arkwright keeps 1,400 props by hand and refuses a computer, on the grounds that the computer would want to be on the air. Twenty favourites, below.

House

The 1908 Stair

The oldest employee. F sharp at full weight. Never oiled.

Ship, recovered

Kindly Light whistle

From the trawler of the same name. Sings in Signal Fires and in dreams.

Interior, 1911

The door

Its latch is the closest thing radio has to a signature.

Kitchen, adapted

Rain tray

The size of a dinner table; fed by a watering can with one hole.

Theatre, surplus

Thunder sheet

Hung from the gallery rail. Struck only in emergencies and finales.

Carved pine

Seagull 1

The credible one. Seagull 2 is for crowd scenes.

Rigging

Creaking bosun's chair

Certified by two coastguards and one sceptic.

Set dressing

Broken radio, prop

Tuned permanently to ourselves. The audience never notices.

Harbour salvage

Foghorn (hand)

Fits in a suitcase; does not fit in the schedule before 9 PM.

Yard

Wheelbarrow of gravel

For beaches. Refilled by season.

The goat

Harbour salvage

Boat horn, twin

Two notes, one mile apart in the mix.

Telegraph key

House

Sand hourglass, minute

Times the dead air. Fifty-nine seconds. The sixtieth is the audience's.

Rain, close

Umbrella on drumskin

For rain that must sound undecided.

Music dept

Cello, mute

Petra's. Also the only instrument allowed in Studio B.

Kettle

Teacups, eleven

Wind machine
