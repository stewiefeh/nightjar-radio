88.9 Gullwrack Point · est. 1948 · drama after dark

The radio never sleeps in the Reach.

Nightjar Radio broadcasts original radio drama from a converted lighthouse at Gullwrack Point. Six series in repertory, one effects bench, forty-two thousand lacquer discs, and a strict rule that the kettle goes on at ten.

How an episode is made

The script arrives, or the pile promotes it.

Casting over one lunch. Three leads, eleven rotating players.

One rehearsal. The pips rehearse separately.

Recorded live to wire, one take, full bench.

Corrections appended as letters, never edits.
