Shortwave

Between midnight and 2 AM the skip opens and Nightjar has been received, at various times, in Iceland, the Azores, and a fishing trawler that wrote to say so in 1979. The letter is framed in the lantern room.

No stream, on principle
There is no internet stream. The board has debated it every year since 1999 and the kettle keeps winning.
